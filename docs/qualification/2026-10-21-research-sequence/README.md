# Whole-record research sequence

These records distinguish implementation checks, an observed learning mechanism,
and task-quality or efficiency evidence. A passing mechanism audit establishes
that experience reached an executed change. It does not establish that the
change helped. Persistent activation and automatic selection of new asset kinds
remain disabled by this work.

The executor is GPT-6 Luna/low and the editor is GPT-6 Sol/high through the local
Codex subscription. Task/probe execution keeps the executor fixed. All failed
search, review, reflection and seed acquisition count. Subscription dollar
values are informational; this archive makes no API billing claim.

## Development comparison

[The frozen selection](development-selection.json) chooses journal and merge
because the prior developer-visible baseline passed 3/4 and 0/4 respectively.
It raises the task horizon to 24 for every arm, uses two repetitions, acquires
two common seed inventories and executes 48 target episodes. These remain two
related-task streams, not 48 independent observations. The source corpus is
synthetic and developer-visible.

The first launcher attempt stopped before authentication, registration or
inference because `not` was applied to a path string. The harness stayed at the
qualified `8f357fa` revision. [The executed driver](development-driver.exs),
[its exact patch](development-driver.patch), [preflight receipt](driver-preflight.json)
and [compatibility evidence](launcher-compatibility.json) record the two
optional-path guard corrections and additional driver provenance. All gates,
budgets, models, graders and stopping rules are unchanged. The driver's SHA-256
is registered before seed dispatch. The tracked fix changes no library or
corpus file; future runs from another revision require their own matching
mechanism receipt. The documentary patch uses zero context: applying it with
`git apply --unidiff-zero` to the frozen script reproduces the registered driver
byte for byte.

The [completed development report](development/report.json) contains all 50
episodes: two seeds and 48 targets. Its [physical-dispatch audit](development/audit.json)
reconciles **2,279 requests and 19,263,954 tokens**, with zero unknown usage,
incomplete episodes or safety failures. One of two seeds passed; the failed
merge seed remained negative experience. Target outcomes were:

Seed acquisition added 26 requests and 222,846 tokens. The registered endpoint
charges all acquisition to updating: **631 requests**, or **5.30 times retry**
and **7.79 times disabled**. Updating used 9.47% fewer requests than the
experience-withheld improver, below the registered 20% saving threshold; only
one of two streams passed in both arms. All journal targets passed in every
arm. Frozen failed the first merge target in both repetitions, and the
experience-withheld arm failed the second merge target in repetition two.
Those failures remain in the report.

Mechanics passed and updating exercised profile adaptation with matching
rollback. **Quality and efficiency remain inconclusive.** There are two
independent streams, and updating matched the passing ordinary/retry controls
with substantially more work. The longer horizon also let the ordinary arm
solve all merge targets, so improvement over the earlier short-horizon
baseline is not learning evidence. These observations support further work on
task headroom and learning overhead; more repetitions of these solved families
cannot establish broad efficacy. No independent confirmation was dispatched.

## Independent confirmation

No independent-confirmation inference is claimed. The available pinned
Terminal-Bench 2.1 checkout contains 89 task manifests. Even treating every task
as an independent stream, the registered assumptions give only a 55.9% lower
bound on joint power for the three quality comparisons. An example with 110
independent streams reaches 80.8%. These are prospective predictions, not
measured efficacy or a claim that published tasks are uncontaminated.

[The feasibility receipt](confirmation-feasibility.json) records zero hosted
confirmation dispatches. A suitable next corpus requires sufficiently many
independent streams, separate unexposed source/grader closures, pinned qualified
images and a fresh exposure attestation. Related variants and shared repository
history cannot inflate the sample. Efficiency power is not inferred from
quality power.

The subsequent [read-only bench inspection](bench-corpus-assessment.json)
checked the available material against that boundary:

| Local material | Available | Confirmation limit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Terminal-Bench | 89 tasks; prepared pools of 49 and 16 | The bench ledger marks the entire suite as development. Recorded controls also found nine reference failures and one no-op pass. |
| Prepared SWE-bench sample | 50 instances across ten repositories | All 50 are marked development by the bench ledger. |
| Full local SWE task metadata | 2,519 task directories across 18 repositories | Remaining exposure is unattested; shared repository history cannot supply 2,519 independent streams. |

The bench already provides official SWE-bench evaluation, immutable image and
evaluator locks, controls and exposure tracking. Its `swe-bench-v1` execution
contract needs a trusted bridge to this study's isolated runtime/evidence path;
its manifest cannot simply be renamed into the study's separate pytest-source
schema. This inspection changed no bench file and made no inference call.
It found useful development infrastructure, not a prepared, adequately powered
unexposed confirmation corpus. Historical controls were inspected, not rerun.
