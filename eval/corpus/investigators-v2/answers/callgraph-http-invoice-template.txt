routes.py maps GET /invoices/{id}.pdf to controllers.invoice_document, which calls render('invoice_document', ..., media='pdf'). registry.template_path prefers the exact media row in config/templates.tsv over the `*` row, so the file is templates/pdf/invoice-2026.tpl, not html/invoice-document.html; templates/pdf/invoice.tpl and templates/legacy/invoice_pdf.tpl are unreferenced by the table.
